<WikiRule num="6.1" title="Farms and entities" sub="The world is large. Be responsible." severity="leve" scope="juego">

<WikiRuleItem code="6.1.a">
  Farms are allowed, but keep animal populations and unnecessary redstone under control.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="6.1.b">

</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="6.1.c">TNT "lazy chunk" contraptions (such as some mining machines) are not allowed.</WikiRuleItem>

</WikiRule>
