<WikiRule num="8.1" title="Trading on the server" sub="Player-to-player trading is open." severity="leve" scope="juego">

<WikiRuleItem code="8.1.a">Players may freely trade items with one another on the server.</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="8.1.b">Clearly labeled shops are welcome.</WikiRuleItem>

</WikiRule>

<WikiRule num="8.2" title="Scams and real-world money" sub="A firm boundary." severity="expulsion" scope="ambos">

<WikiRuleItem code="8.2.a">
  **Selling items, accounts, or services for real-world money (PayPal, crypto, etc.) is prohibited.** This results in
  immediate removal.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="8.2.b">
  Scamming other players (promising something and not delivering) may result in sanctions based on severity.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="8.2.c">Do not use the server to promote outside sales or personal businesses.</WikiRuleItem>

</WikiRule>

<WikiRule num="8.3" title="Unfair competition" sub="In commercial areas." severity="leve" scope="juego">

<WikiRuleItem code="8.3.a">Do not copy another shop's design to deliberately undercut its prices.</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="8.3.b">Respect the server economy; do not artificially inflate prices.</WikiRuleItem>

</WikiRule>
