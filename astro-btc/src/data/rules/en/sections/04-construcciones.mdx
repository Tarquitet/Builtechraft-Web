import WikiRuleItem from '@/components/wiki/WikiRuleItem.astro';

<WikiRule num="4.1" title="Other players' builds" sub="We keep logs of every block." severity="expulsion" scope="juego">

<WikiRuleItem code="4.1.a">
  Destroying or changing another player's build without permission may lead to an investigation and removal from the
  server.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="4.1.b">
  Taking items from another player's chests, even if they are not protected, is a serious breach of trust.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="4.1.c">
  If you caused the damage, cooperate with the restoration. Refusing to help may increase the sanction.
</WikiRuleItem>

</WikiRule>

<WikiRule num="4.2" title="Portals and paths" sub="Keep transportation organized and respect other players." severity="leve" scope="juego">

<WikiRuleItem code="4.2.a">
  If your portal interferes with another base and has no sign identifying you, its owner may remove it.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="4.2.b">
  We recommend adding a sign with coordinates or the owner's name to every portal.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="4.2.c">
  Accidental portal linking caused by nearby chunks is reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
</WikiRuleItem>

</WikiRule>

<WikiRule num="4.3" title="Abandonment and inactivity" sub="We preserve space for active players." severity="leve" scope="juego">

<WikiRuleItem code="4.3.a">
  If you expect to be inactive for a long time, notify staff. Your whitelist will remain active.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="4.3.b">
  After an extended absence without notice, staff may reclaim the area, with advance notice whenever possible.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="4.3.c">Shops in commercial areas may be removed after 21 days of inactivity.</WikiRuleItem>

</WikiRule>
