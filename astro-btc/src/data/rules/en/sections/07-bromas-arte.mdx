<WikiRule num="7.1" title="Permitted pranks" sub="The fun stops when it bothers someone." severity="leve" scope="juego">

<WikiRuleItem code="7.1.a">
  Only prank trusted friends. If you do not know how they will react, do not do it.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="7.1.b">Restore any damage if the affected player asks you to.</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="7.1.c">Do not use fire, TNT, lava, or valuable items in pranks.</WikiRuleItem>

</WikiRule>

<WikiRule num="7.2" title="Art and suggestive content" sub="Creative freedom has limits." severity="grave" scope="juego">

<WikiRuleItem code="7.2.a">Each player may have up to two suggestive artworks.</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="7.2.b">
  Keep them in private areas (basements or rooms). Displaying them publicly may result in sanctions.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="7.2.c">
  If someone sees your content because you did not protect it, you are responsible.
</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="7.2.d">
  Staff has the final say on what counts as art and what is prohibited. These decisions are not open to debate.
</WikiRuleItem>

</WikiRule>

<WikiRule num="7.3" title="Symbols and messages" sub="Keep it appropriate." severity="grave" scope="juego">

<WikiRuleItem code="7.3.a">Do not build offensive symbols or disturbing messages.</WikiRuleItem>

<WikiRuleItem code="7.3.b">
  Publicly visible inappropriate content must be removed and may result in sanctions.
</WikiRuleItem>

</WikiRule>
