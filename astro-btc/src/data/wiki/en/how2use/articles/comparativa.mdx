## A closer look at each option

<WikiAccordion title="Mods — Forge / Fabric" icon="🔧">

Mods are changes installed **on your computer** and/or on the server. They are the most powerful option, but also the most invasive.

**Types of mods:**

- **Client-side:** only on your PC (Sodium, Zan's Minimap, JEI)
- **Server-side:** only on the server
- **Hybrid:** on both sides (most mods that add content)

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Plugins — Spigot / Paper" icon="🔌">

Plugins are installed **only on the server**, so you can join with regular Minecraft.

**Server types:** Spigot (the original), Paper (optimized), Leaves (modern), Purpur (more configuration options).

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Datapacks — pure vanilla" icon="📦">

Datapacks are included **in the world** and change gameplay without modifying the base code. No installation, fully faithful.

</WikiAccordion>

## What did we choose, and why?

We chose **datapacks**: join with regular Minecraft, everyone has the same experience, technical mechanics stay intact, and the world remains stable over time.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="About reliability">

Datapacks and server-side mods can be unreliable **if poorly written**. When built well, everything runs on the server and players do not need to install anything.

</WikiCallout>

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Mods we do recommend">

Optimization mods (Sodium, Lithium) and client-side tools (Zan's Minimap, JEI) are useful and preserve vanilla gameplay, although they must be installed on your computer.

</WikiCallout>
