## Access and accounts

<WikiAccordion title="Do you accept Premium and Non-Premium accounts?">

Yes. Premium accounts sign in with Microsoft; Non-Premium accounts use Ely.by for authentication and skins. See <a href={links.wiki.wikiLaunchers}>launcher information</a> to choose a client.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Do I need to be on the whitelist?">

Yes. This is a private server: request access on Discord and wait for staff approval. It usually takes less than 24 hours. See <a href={links.wiki.wikiWhitelist}>how to request whitelist access</a>.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Why can't I move after joining?">

The server freezes you until you complete the security login. Follow the chat instructions. If you remain frozen, submit a request with your username and a screenshot.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Can I change my username?">

Changing your name may affect your access, inventory, and whitelist. Submit a request before joining with your new name.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Which Minecraft version should I use?">

Always use the server's current native version. Older versions may display blocks incorrectly or become desynchronized. Confirm the current version on Discord. Guides: <a href={links.wiki.wikiLaunchers}>Java / Bedrock</a>.

</WikiAccordion>

## Connection and client

<WikiAccordion title="Where can I find the IP address and port?">

Only use the official Discord channels. These details may change during maintenance. Bedrock players also need the listed port.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Can I play on Bedrock, mobile, or console?">

Yes, through a compatibility bridge. Set the current IP and port by following the Bedrock guide. The server may appear without a ping in the menu; try joining anyway.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Do I need to accept the server resource pack?">

Yes. Enable "Server Resource Packs" when adding Builtechraft. The pack includes required textures; rejecting it may leave some objects missing.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Which launcher should I use?">

Use Prism Launcher with Premium accounts and ElyPrism with Non-Premium Ely.by accounts. Do not use TLauncher. See <a href={links.wiki.wikiLaunchers}>launcher information</a> for details.

</WikiAccordion>

## Mods and performance

<WikiAccordion title="Do I have to install mods?">

No. You can join with a vanilla client. The official modpack is optional and improves performance, navigation, and technical building. Start with the <a href={links.wiki.wikiModpack}>modpack guide</a>.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Which mods can I use?">

You may use the official pack and client-side tools that do not automate actions or reveal hidden information. Check <a href={links.pages.rules}>the rules</a> for the complete list.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Can I use Litematica, MiniHUD, VoxelMap, or Bobby?">

Yes, as part of the official pack. Use Litematica only to view schematics; automatic placement and features that provide an advantage are not allowed.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Can I use shaders and texture packs?">

Yes, as long as they only change visuals. Packs that reveal ores, players, or hidden structures are prohibited. X-Ray effects are violations.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Why does Bobby show old builds?">

Bobby stores a local chunk cache that does not update until you approach the area again. Do not make decisions about a build based only on the cache; check it in person.

</WikiAccordion>

## Rules and community

<WikiAccordion title="Are PvP, stealing, or griefing allowed?">

No. Builtechraft is cooperative: do not take items, alter builds, or block access without permission. Pranks require consent.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Can I use macros, X-Ray, or autoclickers?">

No. Anything that automates actions, reveals hidden information, or alters movement or combat is prohibited (auto-mining, ESP, fly, reach, exploits).

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Are there exceptions for mods or macros?">

Some exceptions are allowed: Tweakeroo is permitted because its "logical" macros do not break game mechanics and only speed up building. It is optional and will not lead to a ban when not used against other players.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Can I use bots or alternate accounts?">

Not to bypass limits or automate activity. Server-authorized fake players are the only controlled exception (for farms and chunk loading).

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="What is the difference between Survival and Creative?">

Survival is for progression and gathering resources; Creative is a workshop for redstone and planning. Respect the purpose of each world.

</WikiAccordion>

## Performance and support

<WikiAccordion title="Why am I lagging or getting disconnected?">

Possible causes include entities, farms, your connection, or maintenance. Try a clean instance, lower your render distance, and close demanding apps.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="When does the server turn on and off?">

It usually starts around 12:00 AM on weekdays and 09:00 AM on weekends (UTC-5). It usually shuts down around 01:00 AM (UTC-5). Maintenance may change this schedule.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="How do I report a bug, player, or technical issue?">

Submit a request in the Discord forum with a clear description, screenshots, coordinates, time, and reproduction steps. Never send passwords or personal information.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="What should I do if the game crashes when I join?">

Check the recommended version and remove unapproved mods. If you use the modpack, create a clean instance. If the problem continues, attach the crash log to a request.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Didn't find your answer?">

Submit a request in <a href={links.social.discord}>our Discord</a> forum and staff will help you.

</WikiCallout>
