Builtechraft began in 2020 as a private server created to bring artistic creativity and engineering together in Minecraft. Here is the timeline of our journey.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="A note about this timeline">

This history is being written from the founder's recollections. Some entries are temporary and were originally added to test the wiki timeline component.

</WikiCallout>
