# Durability Ping

Has your diamond or netherite pickaxe ever broken **while mining obsidian** or deep in a dark cave without torches? This datapack prevents that surprise with a simple visual warning.

## 🛠️ How it works

It works automatically and needs no configuration:

1. When your equipped tool or armor reaches **1 durability point**, your screen briefly flashes **red**.
2. The flash is an immediate warning to repair or switch items **before** it is too late.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="The item must be equipped">

The red flash **only happens** when the item is in your main hand or offhand. A nearly broken pickaxe in your inventory will not trigger the warning. Check your inventory before a long mining trip.

</WikiCallout>

## 💡 Tips

### 1. Carry repair materials

When the red flash appears, it is time to:

- Take out a **portable anvil**, if you have one.
- Use **ingots or materials** to repair the item at a nearby anvil.
- Switch to your **backup tool**.

### 2. Organize your hotbar

Keep these items on your hotbar:

- Your main tool.
- A backup tool of the same type.
- An anvil, if possible.

### 3. Do not ignore the warning

If you ignore the flash and the tool breaks, you may face:

- **Lost time:** you will have to go back for another tool.
- **Lost resources:** breaking in the Nether or End can be costly.
- **Risk of death:** a broken sword or armor can leave you vulnerable in combat.

## ️ Enchantment compatibility

<WikiCallout type="ok" title="Unbreaking">

The datapack **respects** the Unbreaking enchantment. With Unbreaking III, the flash appears later because durability is consumed more slowly. It pairs well with high-level tools.

</WikiCallout>

## 🎯 Critical situations

### Mining obsidian

You are mining obsidian for a Nether portal. Your diamond pickaxe has Unbreaking III but is nearly worn out. The red flash warns you **before** it breaks on the last block and leaves you without a portal.

### Exploring the End

You are fighting the Dragon or exploring End cities. Your sword or bow is nearly broken. The flash gives you time to switch to a backup weapon.

### Automatic farms

If you are AFK at a mob or iron farm, the warning lets you repair the axe or pickaxe before the farm stops working.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Advanced customization">

If you are a server administrator or have permission, some datapacks allow the flash intensity or durability threshold to be adjusted. Ask staff whether this option is available.

</WikiCallout>

## 🔧 Alternative: visible durability bar

If you prefer a more direct display, you can also:

- Enable **Show Item Durability** with F3 + H (Java Edition).
- Use **interface mods** that show exact durability values.

The red flash has one advantage: **it is hard to miss**, even when you are distracted.
