Does your tavern look great while your friends still stand like statues? Want to build an amphitheater or realistic waiting room? **Sit Anywhere** lets you sit on almost any solid block.

## ️ How to use it

The system is intuitive and needs no unusual commands. It is designed not to interfere with normal block interactions.

1. Approach a block you want to sit on: stairs, slabs, fences, walls, beds, or even a full block such as stone or wood.
2. Hold **Shift (Crouch)**.
3. **Right-click** the block.

Your character will sit on the edge or center of the block.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Why Shift + right-click?">

Crouching keeps the datapack from interfering with blocks that already have a right-click action, such as chests, beds, or crafting tables. Otherwise, the game would not know whether you want to open the block or sit on it.

</WikiCallout>

## How to stand up

There are two easy options:

- **Jump:** press Space.
- **Walk:** move with WASD.

## 🏗️ Ideas for builders and decorators

- **Taverns and cafés:** place chairs using stairs or slabs around tables so players can sit together.
- **Amphitheaters and cinemas:** build seating with stone or wooden stairs for live events or map-based films.
- **AFK areas:** add a bench at spawn or your base where players can sit while waiting for friends.
- **Parks:** use slabs and fences to create realistic park benches.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="Be careful around voids and lava">

You can sit on _any_ block, including one floating in midair or above lava. If an unsafe block is broken or falls (like sand), **you may fall into the void or burn**. Make sure your seat is stable.

</WikiCallout>

## ⚙️ Compatibility and limits

- **Transparent blocks:** glass, leaves, and torches are not considered solid seating surfaces.
- **Complex hitboxes:** on fences and walls, your character sits on top. It may look like you are floating, but it works.
- **Multiplayer:** only one player can sit in the exact same space. Respect personal space.
