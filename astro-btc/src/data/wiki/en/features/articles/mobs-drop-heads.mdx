# Mobs Drop Heads

This datapack adds a simple but valuable mechanic for builders and decorators: **mobs can drop their heads when killed**.

## How it works

When you kill a mob (zombie, skeleton, Creeper, Enderman, and more), there is a **small chance** it will drop its head as a collectible item.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Base drop chance">

The chance is intentionally low (about 2.5% by default), which makes each head valuable. Do not be discouraged if you do not get many at first; that is part of the challenge.

</WikiCallout>

## ️ Tips for farming heads

<WikiCallout type="ok" title="Use Looting">

The datapack works without enchantments, but a sword with **Looting III** significantly increases your chance of getting heads. It is the most efficient way to farm them.

</WikiCallout>

### Mobs that drop heads

- **Zombie** → Zombie Head
- **Skeleton** → Skeleton Skull
- **Creeper** → Creeper Head
- **Enderman** → Enderman Head
- **Wither Skeleton** → Wither Skeleton Skull (already available in vanilla; this datapack adds other mobs too)
- **Zombified Piglin** → Piglin Head
- **Villager** → Villager Head (very rare)

## 🏗️ Creative uses

Mob heads are more than trophies; they are versatile building materials.

### 1. Statues and decoration

Place heads on fence posts, walls, or armor stands to create:

- **Custom NPCs** for villages.
- **Hunting trophies** in your main hall.
- **Horror details** in dungeons or haunted houses.

### 2. Decorative mob farms

Use heads to label sections of a mob farm or create a hall of fame featuring the mobs you have farmed most.

### 3. Redstone systems

Mob heads can be used in advanced redstone mechanisms:

- As decorative blocks that also form part of circuits.
- To create "head readers" with armor stands.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="Player heads">

This datapack does **not** affect player heads. To obtain player heads, you need special commands or the "Player Head Drops" datapack, if it is installed.

</WikiCallout>

## 💡 Advanced tip: Wither Skeleton farms

If you have access to the Nether, **Wither Skeleton farms** are a reliable way to collect heads because:

1. Wither Skeletons already have a vanilla head drop chance (2.5%).
2. This datapack gives other mobs the same chance.
3. Combined with Looting III, you can collect multiple heads per hour.
