Repeated trips between a distant farm and your main base are one of Minecraft's most tedious tasks. **Revault** adds a useful item: a **chest you can carry in your inventory**.

## 🛠️ How it works

It is almost as simple as placing and picking up a regular chest:

1. **Get a Revault:** craft one, or receive one depending on the server configuration.
2. **Place it:** right-click the ground to place it like a regular chest.
3. **Store items:** open its interface and deposit the resources you have collected.
4. **Pick it up:** right-click the placed chest **with an empty hand** (or the Revault item, depending on the configuration).
5. The chest disappears from the ground and returns to your inventory, **keeping everything inside**.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="💡 Usage tip">

You can carry several Revaults and rename them with an anvil (for example, "Wood," "Stone," or "Nether loot") to organize long expeditions.

</WikiCallout>

## 🚨 Critical safety warning

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="This is not an Ender Chest">

Unlike an Ender Chest, a Revault's contents are **not linked to your account**. If you die while carrying it, **it drops like any other item**. It can burn in lava or despawn if not recovered. **Never** use it to store your most valuable items (diamonds, Netherite) in a high-risk area.

</WikiCallout>

## 🎯 Best use cases

### 1. Nether or End expeditions

Instead of making ten trips to bring back gold, quartz, or End Stone, fill your Revault, pick it up, and make one trip back to safety.

### 2. Cleaning out distant farms

If an iron or villager farm is thousands of blocks from your base, use a Revault to collect items from its output chests and bring them back at once.

### 3. Building in difficult terrain

When building on a mountain or over the ocean without room for temporary chests, use a Revault as portable storage.

## ⚙️ Compatibility and rules

- **Protected areas:** normal block-protection rules apply. You cannot place or pick up a Revault in another player's claim or at spawn without permission.
- **Shulker Boxes:** Revault complements Shulker Boxes. Use Shulkers for bulk storage at your base and Revault for active transport.
