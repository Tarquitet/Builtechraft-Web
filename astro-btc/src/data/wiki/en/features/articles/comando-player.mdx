At **Builtechraft**, fake players (bots) are allowed for technical farms and chunk loading. They are powered by Carpet Mod and its `/player` command.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="What is a fake player?">

A server-controlled entity that simulates a real player. It can move, look around, break blocks, right-click, and more, but it cannot chat or use custom inventories.

</WikiCallout>

## 🤖 What is /player for?

- **Automatic AFK farms:** leave a bot at a farm without staying connected.
- **Chunk loading:** keep areas loaded while no real players are nearby.
- **Redstone testing:** simulate a player to activate mechanisms.
- **Collaborative building:** have a bot hold items or repeat actions.

## 🎭 Shadow players

A **shadow** is an independent entity that mirrors a real player's behavior in real time.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="When should I use shadow?">

Use it when you need to **replicate the behavior of a real player**, for example:

- Testing complex mechanisms that require multiple players.
- Debugging redstone from multiple perspectives.
- Farms that depend on replicated player behavior.

</WikiCallout>

## ⚠️ Rules and limitations

<WikiCallout type="danger" title="Important restrictions">

- **Valid names:** use Mojang Premium usernames only; offline or invented names are not allowed.
- **Location:** bots may only be summoned where players are connected. **Do not summon them in uninhabited areas.**
- **Permissions:** Survival players cannot use selectors (@p, @e, @a). Use specific names only.
- **Quantity:** do not abuse bots or shadows; each uses server resources.

</WikiCallout>

## ✅ Allowed vs. ❌ prohibited

<WikiAccordion title="✅ Allowed">

- Iron farms with an AFK bot near where you are playing.
- Keeping crop farm chunks loaded while you are in the area.
- Testing redstone mechanisms at your base.
- XP farms with bots attacking mobs in controlled areas.
- Bots following players at spawn or in community areas.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="❌ Prohibited">

- Using bots to mine blocks automatically (macros/auto-mining).
- Creating bot armies for PvP.
- Using bots to bypass server activity rules.
- Summoning bots in distant areas with no connected players.
- Using offline or invented names.
- Summoning bots in another player's area without permission.
- Using entity selectors (@p, @e, and similar).

</WikiAccordion>

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

<WikiAccordion title="The bot does not appear">

- Check that you have permission to use the command.
- Make sure the name is a valid Mojang username.
- Confirm there are players connected in the area.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="The bot does not move">

- Use `move forward` instead of absolute coordinates.
- Check whether the bot is stuck in blocks.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="The bot disappears">

- Bots are removed when the server restarts.
- Summon them again after each restart.

</WikiAccordion>
