Create decorative heads of objects, animals, or players. The server datapack works without commands or resource packs.

<WikiVideo youtube="9-5MfSUUQ6g" caption="Video: Decorative heads" />

## 🎨 Prepare blank heads

To create a custom head, start with a blank base. They are inexpensive and easy to craft.

- **Allowed materials:** regular, white, light gray, or brown terracotta.
- **Crafting:** arrange eight terracotta blocks in a ring on a crafting table, like a chest or furnace recipe.
- **Result:** you receive **four blank heads**.

With blank heads and a **Book and Quill**, choose one of the following methods.

<WikiTabs>

<WikiTab label="Method 1: Heads by code">

Use this method for specific decorative objects such as burgers, coffee cups, mini TVs, or animals.

1. Visit [Minecraft-Heads.com](https://minecraft-heads.com/).
2. Browse the categories and choose a head.
3. Scroll to the bottom of its page, find **For Developers**, and copy all the text in the **Value** field (the long code made of letters and numbers).
4. Return to the game and open a **Book and Quill**. Paste the code on the first page. **Do not sign the book**; leave it open.

**Apply the code:**

- Put the blank heads in your **offhand** (press <WikiKbd>F</WikiKbd>).
- Hold the book with the pasted code in your **main hand**.
- Press the **swap-hands key (<WikiKbd>F</WikiKbd>)** to switch items.
- The book consumes the code, becomes blank, and turns your heads into the selected design.

</WikiTab>

<WikiTab label="Method 2: Heads by username">

Use a username to get the head of a friend, creator, or specific character.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="Important requirement">

The name must belong to an **original Mojang Premium account**; Ely.by-only names are not supported. Verify names at [NameMC](https://namemc.com/).

</WikiCallout>

1. Get the exact username of the Premium player.
2. Open a **Book and Quill** in-game. Leave its pages empty.
3. Select **Sign** directly.
4. In the book title field, **enter the exact Premium username** and sign it.

**Apply the name:**

- Put the blank heads in your **offhand**.
- Hold the signed book in your **main hand**.
- Press the **swap-hands key (<WikiKbd>F</WikiKbd>)**.
- Your heads will become that player's head.

</WikiTab>
