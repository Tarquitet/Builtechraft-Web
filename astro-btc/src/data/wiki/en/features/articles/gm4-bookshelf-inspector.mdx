# GM4 Bookshelf Inspector

Have you ever built a wall of bookshelves for enchanted books, then forgotten which of the fifty shelves contains _Mending_ or _Fortune III_?

In vanilla Minecraft, the only way to find out is to break a bookshelf, take out the books, and put it back. That is bad for decoration and wastes time. **GM4 Bookshelf Inspector** solves this elegantly.

## 🛠️ How to use it

It is simple enough to feel like magic, and requires no special item or command.

1. Approach any **Bookshelf** block that contains books.
2. Make sure your hand is **empty** (or holding an item that does not interact with blocks).
3. **Right-click** the bookshelf.

A menu will show the three enchanted books stored in that specific block.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="💡 Organization tip">

Consider sorting books by type: one section for armor enchantments, another for tools, and another for bows. Then the Inspector helps you find exactly the right area.

</WikiCallout>

## 🏗️ Benefits for builders and collectors

### 1. No destruction

Inspect bookshelves without breaking them. Your library, enchanting room, or storage wall stays intact.

### 2. Save time

Instead of breaking and replacing shelves, find the book you need in seconds.

### 3. Useful on multiplayer servers

If you share a base with friends, leave notes or written books inside shelves. Everyone can use the Inspector to read them without removing them, turning bookshelves into a mailbox or notice board.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="⚠️ Inspection only">

This datapack is an **Inspector**. It lets you see what is inside, but **does not let you add or remove books** through the interface. Break the bookshelf to move its contents. It is designed for viewing, not inventory management.

</WikiCallout>

## Creative uses

- **Enchantment museums:** display rare books in bookshelves. Visitors can inspect them without taking them.
- **Clues and puzzles:** hide written clues in specific bookshelves in an adventure map or your base.
