# Armor Statues Datapack

In vanilla Minecraft, armor stands are rigid mannequins: they stand upright, face forward, and keep their arms at their sides.

The **Armor Statues** datapack removes those limitations. Rotate body parts, resize the stand, or hide parts to turn a simple mannequin into a professional decoration tool.

## 🛠️ How to use it

The exact interaction may vary slightly with the server configuration, but the general process is:

1. **Place a regular armor stand** on the ground.
2. **Equip it** with the armor or items you want it to hold.
3. **Use the editing tool:** our server usually uses a specific item (such as a **stick renamed** to "Editor" or a **magic wand**) or an authorized command.
4. **Interact:** hold the tool or crouch and right-click different parts of the stand (head, arms, legs) to open the editing menu or rotate the selected part.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="💡 Not sure which tool to use?">

If you do not have the editing item, ask in the `#support` channel on Discord or check the builder starter kit. Staff will be happy to help.

</WikiCallout>

## 🎨 What can you do with it?

This datapack opens up many creative possibilities:

- **Dynamic poses:** make a stand run, jump, fight, or sit (combine it with _Sit Anywhere_).
- **Scaling:** create giant statues for a city entrance or tiny figures and pets for your base.
- **Invisible parts:** hide the body and leave a floating helmet, or show only the arms holding a sword. Great for displaying weapons on walls.
- **Precise rotation:** adjust the head or arms by exact degrees to make them face the right direction.

## ️ Ideas for builders

- **Village NPCs:** create custom villagers or guards for shops and market stalls. Give one a name tag and you have a living NPC.
- **Action scenes:** stage a tavern fight or place sleeping statues in beds.
- **Shop mannequins:** display clothing or armor products in your server shop.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="⚠️ Beware of entity lag">

Armor stands count as **entities**. This datapack is optimized, but hundreds of stands in one chunk (for example, in a large museum) can cause lag nearby. Use only as many as your decoration needs.

</WikiCallout>

## 🔧 Emergency reset

If a statue ends up at an odd angle or sinks into the ground, you do not have to break it and start over. Use the reset command (usually `/as reset`) or left-click while crouching, depending on the configuration, to restore its original pose.
