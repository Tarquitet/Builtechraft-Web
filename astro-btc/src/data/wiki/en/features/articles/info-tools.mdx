# Info Tools

Have you ever found an unusual block and wondered what it is called? Or needed to check a area's light level to prevent mob spawns, but did not want to open the debug screen (F3)?

The **Info Tools** datapack adds special items that act like "data flashlights." They display useful technical information about what you are looking at.

## 🛠️ How to use it

The system is intuitive and requires no commands.

1. **Get the tool:** find the designated item in Creative inventory, or ask staff on Discord. It is usually a **stick renamed** to "Editor" or "Info Stick," or a special compass.
2. **Hold the tool:** place it in your main hand.
3. **Aim and look:** point at the block or entity (mob, player, or animal) you want to inspect.
4. **Read the information:** details appear at the top of your screen in the action bar or in chat, depending on the configuration.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="💡 Do you need the tool?">

If you are in Survival and do not know how to get it, ask in the `#support` channel on Discord. Staff can provide one or tell you the crafting recipe if it is enabled.

</WikiCallout>

## 📊 What information does it show?

The tool reveals different details depending on whether you are looking at a block or an entity:

### When looking at a block

- **Block name:** its exact ID (for example, `minecraft:deepslate_iron_ore`).
- **Coordinates:** the block's exact X, Y, and Z position.
- **Light level:** useful for checking how much light a block receives (0–15). At 0 or below 7, watch out for zombies.
- **Hardness and resistance:** how long it takes to break, and whether it resists explosions.

### When looking at an entity (mobs, players, items)

- **Entity type:** for example, `minecraft:zombie` or `minecraft:item`.
- **Health:** how many hearts it has left.
- **Custom name:** whether the mob has a name tag.
- **UUID:** the entity's unique identifier (advanced users and staff).

## 🎯 Everyday uses

### 1. Safe building (preventing mob spawns)

When lighting a base or cave, use Info Tools to check the **exact light level** of the floor instead of guessing. If it is 7 or lower, place another torch. No more surprise creepers.

### 2. Identifying technical blocks

If you are copying a build from YouTube and cannot tell whether a block is Stone or Polished Andesite, point the tool at it to identify it instantly.

### 3. Precise locations

Need to share the coordinates of a bug or structure with a friend or staff? Point at the block and read the coordinates on screen. It is faster than opening F3 and searching for small numbers.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="⚠️ Use responsibly">

This tool is meant to help, not to be spammed. Scanning hundreds of entities in a small area, such as a large mob farm, can flood your screen with text. Use it when you need the information.

</WikiCallout>
