Explore the worlds in your browser and share public locations. The web map requires no mods or commands.

## 🗺️ Open the web map

There is nothing to download; the map works in your browser on both computers and mobile devices.

<WikiSteps
  steps={[
    { title: 'Open the official website', text: 'Go to builtechraft.tarquitet.com.' },
    { title: 'Open the map section', text: 'Choose Maps from the navigation menu.' },
    { title: 'Choose a world', text: 'Select Survival or Creative.' },
    { title: 'View the map', text: 'Select View Map.' },
  ]}
/>

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Please wait">

Depending on how much of the world has been explored, the map may take a few seconds to load.

</WikiCallout>

## 🎮 Map controls

The interface is similar to Google Maps, with additional tools.

<WikiAccordion title="Basic controls">

- **Move:** left-click and drag, or swipe on mobile.
- **Zoom:** use the mouse wheel or pinch the screen.
- **Rotate the camera:** right-click and drag.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Side toolbar">

- **3D / 2D perspective:** switch between a flat top-down view and 3D (3D uses more resources).
- **Day and night:** change lighting with the Sun or Moon icon.
- **Players:** view who is connected; select a name to jump to their live coordinates.
- **Markers:** see public areas, bases, and farms shared by players.
- **Dimensions:** switch between the Overworld, Nether, and End.

</WikiAccordion>

## 📍 Create public markers

Built a public farm or market and want everyone to find it on the map? Create interactive _waypoints_ in-game.

### Step 1: Gather the materials

You will need:

- **A banner** in any color.
- **An anvil and experience** to rename it.
- **An empty locator map** (one compass surrounded by eight paper).

### Step 2: Name the banner

Use an anvil to give the banner a title, such as _Iron Farm_ or _Tarquitet's Base_.

### Step 3: Place and register it

<WikiSteps
  steps={[
    {
      title: 'Place the banner',
      text: 'Go to the exact location you want to mark and place the renamed banner on the ground or a wall.',
    },
    {
      title: 'Open the map',
      text: 'Right-click the air with your empty locator map so it starts drawing the terrain.',
    },
    { title: 'Register the marker', text: 'Stand near the banner and right-click it with the open map.' },
  ]}
/>

Your paper map will show a small icon in the banner's color and display its name.

### Step 4: Sync it to the web map

When you right-click the banner with the map, the server detects the interaction and **automatically syncs the location to the web map**. Reload the page to see your public waypoint.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Decoration tip">

Place the map in an Item Frame near the banner to create a static minimap on the wall of your base.

</WikiCallout>
