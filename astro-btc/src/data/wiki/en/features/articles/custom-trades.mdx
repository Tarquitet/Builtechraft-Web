# Custom Trades

In vanilla Minecraft, finding a _Mending_ or _Efficiency V_ book can be frustrating. Villagers may offer dozens of books you do not need, such as Blast Protection III.

The **Custom Trades** datapack expands villager offers, especially for **Librarians**, making trading halls more useful and varied.

## 🛠️ What exactly changes?

The datapack modifies the villagers' internal loot tables.

- **More variety:** Librarians can offer enchantments that are extremely rare or unavailable at early trade levels in the base game.
- **Better progression:** Emeralds and time spent breeding villagers feel worthwhile instead of wasted.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="100% vanilla friendly">

This datapack **adds no new items** and does not break the game's balance. It reorganizes existing Minecraft content to make trading fairer and more enjoyable.

</WikiCallout>

## 💡 The ultimate strategy: curing zombified villagers

To get the most from this datapack, use a classic Minecraft technique:

### 1. The curing discount

When a zombie infects a villager and you cure it with a **Golden Apple** and a **Potion of Weakness**, it may offer **huge discounts**—sometimes one emerald for a book that used to cost sixty.

### 2. The reroll cycle

If a Librarian does not offer the book you want (such as _Mending_):

1. **Break its lectern** before you make the first trade.
2. Place the lectern again.
3. The villager will lose its profession and generate new trades.
4. Repeat until the desired book appears.
5. **Make the first trade immediately!** A single paper or book trade locks its offers permanently.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="Be careful with the first trade">

If you buy anything from the villager—even one paper for one emerald—**its offers are locked permanently**. Breaking the lectern will no longer change them. Make sure the book is the one you want.

</WikiCallout>

## 🏗️ Designing a trading hall

Since the trades are better, plan your hall with room to expand:

- **1x2 modules:** leave enough space between villagers so a zombie cannot infect neighboring villagers when you cure one intentionally.
- **Lighting:** use blocks that prevent mob spawns, such as slabs or carpets, on the cell floors.
- **Beds:** each villager needs a bed to claim an area and reproduce if you want to expand the hall.
