Travel between distant locations without `/warp` or `/home`. **LodestoneTP** uses lodestones and linked compasses to preserve the Survival experience.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="🧭 The evolution of travel">

Introduced in the iconic **1.16 Nether Update**, lodestones changed Minecraft's navigation system. **LodestoneTP** takes this further: link a compass to a lodestone and the datapack reads its stored data to let you travel instantly. It rewards players for gathering valuable materials without using cheats.

</WikiCallout>

## ⚖️ Fast-travel limits

To keep this mechanic balanced rather than making it feel like a cheat command, the datapack has some limits:

- **Resource cost:** Fast travel may consume the compass or experience levels, depending on the current difficulty settings. Save it for when you need it.
- **Dimension restriction:** You can only teleport to a lodestone in the same dimension (Overworld to Overworld, Nether to Nether). Cross-dimension travel is canceled.
- **Destroyed lodestones:** If someone or a Creeper breaks the destination lodestone, the link is lost and teleporting there stops working.

## 💡 Tips for builders and engineers

<WikiAccordion title="Rename your compasses">

Use an anvil to give linked compasses clear names such as _Main Base_ or _Slime Farm_. You will know where each one leads.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Build a portal hub">

If you have a megaproject, place several lodestones in a central room at your base to organize your fast-travel network.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="The Ender Chest trick">

Carry an Ender Chest and store all your Lodestone Compasses inside. You will have quick access to your logistics network without filling your main inventory.

</WikiAccordion>
