At Builtechraft, we believe in being transparent. This article explains **how we manage modifications** and the settings that make the server work.

## 🔄 Update management

Updating a technical server is more involved than pressing a button. We maintain an **Update Control Panel** in Google Sheets that tracks everything:

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Three golden rules">

1. **Status of every essential mod**, including compatibility with the next Minecraft version.
2. **Automatic progress tracking:** if critical mods are missing, the panel marks the update "NO" in red.
3. **Safety first:** we never update until security and optimization mods are ready. We would rather wait than risk corrupting the world.

</WikiCallout>
