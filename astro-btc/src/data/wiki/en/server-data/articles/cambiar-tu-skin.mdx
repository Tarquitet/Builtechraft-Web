<WikiCallout type="warn" title="Current method">

Players can change their skin on the same website where they created their account or launcher profile, **without using commands**. The commands below are only for emergencies when the server is in offline mode.

</WikiCallout>

Change your appearance through your Premium or Ely.by account. The server loads your skin after you sign in successfully.

## 🌐 Change your skin (current method)

The server reads your appearance directly from the external account provider you use.

<WikiTabs>

<WikiTab label="Premium players">

Sign in to [Minecraft.net](https://www.minecraft.net/) and change your skin there. It will update when you join the server.

**Alternative:** Most modern launchers (Prism Launcher, XMCL) let you manage accounts and upload skin files from their settings menus.

</WikiTab>

<WikiTab label="Non-Premium players">

Go to [Ely.by](https://ely.by/), sign in, and upload a skin or custom cape. The server's Alternative Auth system will load it automatically.

**Alternative:** Use your launcher's account management tools.

</WikiTab>

<WikiTab label="Client-side mods">

Many players install mods to manage their appearance:

- **Essentials:** a popular mod for changing skins and cosmetics through an in-game menu.
- **Other visual mods:** client-side skin tools compatible with your Minecraft version.

</WikiTab>

</WikiTabs>

## ️ Backup commands (offline mode only)

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Emergencies only">

These commands work only if the server enters **offline mode** because both Ely.by and Mojang authentication are unavailable. Under normal conditions, you do not need them.

</WikiCallout>

<WikiAccordion title="1. Copy another account's skin">

<WikiCommand command="/skin set <Name>" description="Applies the skin of the Premium account you specify." />

<WikiCommand command="/skin set by <Name>" description="Applies the skin of an Ely.by user. (Java only)." />

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="2. Load a skin from a URL">

<WikiCommand
  command="/skin set web classic (URL)"
  description="Loads a classic (Steve) skin from a URL ending in .png."
/>

<WikiCommand command="/skin set web slim (URL)" description="Loads a slim (Alex) skin from a URL ending in .png." />

Find a skin on MinecraftSkins, upload it to Discord or Imgur, right-click it, choose "Copy image address," and paste the URL inside the parentheses.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="3. Clear or reset your skin">

<WikiCommand command="/skin clear" description="Removes the current skin and displays Steve or Alex." />

<WikiCommand command="/skin reset" description="Restores the skin linked to your account." />

</WikiAccordion>
