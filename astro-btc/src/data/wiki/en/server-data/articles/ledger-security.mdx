# Ledger: the server's guardian

**Ledger** is a security and logging mod that acts like an invisible, ever-present security camera across the server. It records **every action**: placing or breaking blocks, opening chests, moving items, killing entities, and more.

Its goal is simple: protect your builds and items, and make sure malicious actions can be reversed and addressed.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="🚨 THE GOLDEN RULE: DO NOT RETALIATE">

If you return to your base and find something stolen or destroyed:

1. **Do not touch anything** in the affected area, so the logs remain clear.
2. **Do not look for the culprit** or take justice into your own hands.
3. **Open a Discord ticket** right away and include the coordinates.

Taking justice into your own hands is **strictly prohibited** and may result in the same or a more severe sanction than the original offense. Let staff use Ledger to investigate.

</WikiCallout>

## 🛠️ How does it work?

Whenever someone interacts with the world, Ledger stores a record of that action in its database:

- **Who:** the player's exact username.
- **What:** the block broken, item taken, or entity killed.
- **When:** the exact date and time, down to the second.
- **Where:** precise X, Y, and Z coordinates.

## ️ What does staff do when you open a ticket?

When you report an incident, administrators use Ledger commands to investigate:

1. **Inspect:** review the history of the affected area to see who was there and what they did.
2. **Rollback:** if theft or _griefing_ is confirmed, use Ledger to **undo the culprit's actions**. Broken blocks are restored and stolen items are returned to your chest or dropped nearby.
3. **Sanction:** the responsible player receives a sanction under the server rules, from a warning to a permanent ban.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="💡 Can I use Ledger myself?">

Ledger lookup commands (such as `/ledger` or `/lg`) are **restricted to staff**. This prevents players from spying on others or overwhelming the server with mass queries. If you need help, open a ticket.

</WikiCallout>

## ️ Attempts to evade the system

Trying to erase logs, exploit the mod, or coordinate theft to avoid records will not work in the long run.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="Zero tolerance">

Any attempt to manipulate, evade, or exploit bugs to prevent Ledger from recording actions results in an **immediate permanent ban**, with no appeal. The system is robust and always leaves a trace.

</WikiCallout>

## 🤝 Community and trust

Ledger lets you build a megaproject, store diamonds in a chest in the middle of the forest, or leave tools at a distant farm **without fear**.

Trust the system, respect other players' work, and remember: at Builtechraft, enforcement is quick, precise, and automatic.
