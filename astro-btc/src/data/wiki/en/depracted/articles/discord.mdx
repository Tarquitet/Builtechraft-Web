Discord is Builtechraft’s meeting place. Set up your channels, read announcements, and check the server status.

<WikiVideo youtube="mKESLTeJY9Y" caption="Video: Our Discord" />

## 🎧 The community hub

We know there are thousands of Minecraft servers, and we appreciate that you chose Builtechraft. To us, it is more than a map: it is a place to learn and spend time together.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Optional, but recommended">

Joining Discord is **optional**, but we recommend it. It is our main tool for keeping things organized, sharing news, and building a friendly community where the goal is to relax and play.

</WikiCallout>

## 🛠️ Set up channels and roles

Discord has a built-in **Channels & Roles** feature in the upper-left area of the server. We recommend using it when you first join:

<WikiAccordion title="Onboarding questions">

- **What platform do you play on?** Java, Bedrock, and more.
- **What do you enjoy?** Redstone, building, exploration.
- **Where are you from?** Help the community understand time zones.
- **Which channels do you want to see?** Hide sections that do not interest you.

_Note: Let Discord manage these preferences automatically instead of muting categories manually._

</WikiAccordion>

## 🤝 Introduce yourself

Once you have your roles, stop by the **Introductions** channel. It is not required, but we are a close-knit community and enjoy learning who we play with:

- Who are you, and what do you do?
- What made you interested in Builtechraft?
- What would you like to build?

Read other introductions and say hello. It is a great way to break the ice.

## 📚 Important channels

<WikiAccordion title="Key areas">

- **Support and help:** report a bug, missing item, or technical question through the forum, like a GitHub issue.
- **Guides:** a collection of essential videos and tutorials.
- **Progress and community:** share build photos and ask for design advice.
- **Suggestions:** propose optimization mods, useful datapacks, or event ideas.
- **Off-topic and spam:** memes, other games, and casual chat without flooding the main channels.

</WikiAccordion>

## 🤖 Check whether the server is online

The server is hosted on a local machine and may depend on external conditions. To avoid asking repeatedly:

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Two status bots">

Two dedicated bots appear in the Discord member list: one for Creative and one for Survival.

- **Green (online):** the server is open and running.
- **Gray (offline):** the server is shut down for rest, maintenance, or network issues.

Staff will announce any major incident through an official announcement.

</WikiCallout>
