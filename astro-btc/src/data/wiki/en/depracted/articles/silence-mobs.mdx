This datapack lets you mute individual mobs.

## 🛠️ How to use it

1. Get a Name Tag and an anvil.
2. Rename the tag exactly to `silence_me`.
3. Use the tag on the mob with a right-click.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="Exact spelling required">

Use lowercase letters and an underscore. `silence_me` is the only valid format.

</WikiCallout>
