## What the list includes

<WikiAccordion title="Optimization and quality-of-life mods">

Tools focused on client performance and quality of life, including:

- **Client optimization** (Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore)
- **Visual improvements** (shaders, minimaps)
- **Quality of life** (JEI, Mod Menu, expanded inventories)
- **Extended rendering** (Bobby for viewing distant chunks)

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Technical datapacks">

Native server behavior tweaks that preserve the natural Survival progression:

- **Lodestone teleportation** (LodestoneTP)
- **Sitting, riding, and crawling**
- **Custom decorative heads**
- **Carpet Mod commands** (/player, /shadow)
- **Public waypoints on the web map**

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Recommended client-side mods">

These are included in the official modpack. If you play vanilla, you may install them yourself:

- **Sodium** + **Lithium** + **FerriteCore** (optimization)
- **Bobby** (view chunks beyond the server's render distance)
- **Xaero's Minimap** or **VoxelMap** (minimap)
- **JEI / REI** (crafting recipes)
- **Litematica** (view schematics only; no automatic placement)

</WikiAccordion>

## Mod policy

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="Allowed mods">

- Optimization and quality-of-life mods included in the official pack.
- Client-side tools that do not automate actions or reveal hidden information.
- Shaders and visual resource packs without X-Ray features.

</WikiCallout>

<WikiCallout type="danger" title="Prohibited mods">

- X-Ray or packs that reveal ores.
- Auto-mining, autoclickers, and macros.
- ESP, fly, reach, and killaura.
- Anything that gives an unfair advantage over other players.

</WikiCallout>
