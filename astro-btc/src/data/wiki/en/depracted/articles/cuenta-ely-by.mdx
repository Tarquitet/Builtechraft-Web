Ely.by is an alternative authentication service that lets Non-Premium players use custom skins and connect to servers such as Builtechraft.

## Why do you need Ely.by?

<WikiCallout type="info">

If you have a **Premium (Microsoft/Mojang)** account, you do not need Ely.by. This guide is only for **Non-Premium** accounts.

Builtechraft uses **Alternative Auth** to verify your Ely.by account when you connect. You cannot join without configuring Ely.by.

</WikiCallout>

## Configure Ely.by in your launcher

After creating your Ely.by account, link it to your launcher:

<WikiAccordion title="ElyPrism Launcher">

1. Open ElyPrism Launcher.
2. Select Accounts in the upper-right corner.
3. Choose Add Ely.by account.
4. Enter your Ely.by email and password.
5. Select Sign in.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Prism Launcher (with plugin)">

The standard Prism Launcher does not support Ely.by natively. Use **ElyPrism** (a Prism fork with Ely.by support) or install the **Ely.by Auth** plugin if one is available for your version.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="Modrinth App">

1. Open Modrinth App.
2. Go to Settings → Accounts.
3. Select Add account → Ely.by.
4. Enter your credentials.

</WikiAccordion>

## Verify that it works

After linking your Ely.by account:

<WikiCallout type="success">

- Your launcher should show your username and skin.
- When you connect to Builtechraft, the server will verify your account automatically.
- You do not need to enter a password in-game; Alternative Auth handles authentication.

</WikiCallout>

## Common issues

<WikiAccordion title="Invalid session when connecting">

If you see "Invalid Session":

1. Close the game completely.
2. Sign out of Ely.by in your launcher.
3. Sign in again with your email and password.
4. Try connecting again.

This can happen when the authentication token expires.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="My skin is not showing in-game">

- Make sure you uploaded a skin to your ely.by profile.
- Check that the model (Classic/Slim) matches the one used by the game.
- Wait a few minutes; skins may take time to synchronize.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="I forgot my password">

1. Go to [ely.by](https://ely.by/).
2. Select Forgot password?
3. Enter your email and follow the recovery instructions.

</WikiAccordion>
