Builtechraft is a small project maintained with care by a dedicated team. We are not a company; we are players who love this server.

## Who we are

The core team currently consists of about **three trusted people**, including founder Tarquitet. They handle:

- Whitelist approvals
- Discord moderation
- Technical support
- Server development and maintenance

<WikiCallout type="info" title="No public staff roster">

For safety and stability, we do not publish staff members' full names on this page. You can identify them by their roles on Discord.

</WikiCallout>

## How to help

If you would like to contribute to the server:

<WikiAccordion title="Ways to contribute">

- **Share ideas** in the Discord suggestions channel.
- **Help with community events** when they are organized.
- **Report bugs** or technical issues in the support forum.
- **Contribute to this wiki** (see <WikiLink to="escribir-articulos">how to write articles</WikiLink>).

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiCallout type="ok" title="The community is part of the team">

Every player who respects the rules and contributes positively is part of the team, even without a Discord role.

</WikiCallout>
