In vanilla Minecraft, Ghasts can feel like slow, heavy balloons. They get stuck under cave ceilings, drift lazily, and make Ghast Tear farms frustrating to build.

The **Speed Happy Ghast** datapack, together with _Ghast Pedal_, adjusts their AI so these floating mobs move more quickly and smoothly.

## 🛠️ What changes?

1. **Higher speed:** Ghasts travel through the air significantly faster.
2. **Better pathfinding:** They are less likely to stick in corners or bounce against Netherrack, and move more directly toward players or spawn areas.
3. **Smoother movement:** Their animations and turns feel more fluid, making combat more dynamic.

## 🏗️ For farm builders

If you want to build a **Ghast Tear farm**, this datapack can help:

- **Higher efficiency:** faster Ghasts reach the killing chamber sooner.
- **Less entity lag:** they are less likely to remain stuck in distant chunks or high ceilings.
- **More compact designs:** improved movement may reduce the need for large water streams or ice systems.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="Building tip">

Ghasts still need a 5x5x4-block space to spawn. Make sure your farm platforms have the right ceiling height.

</WikiCallout>

## ⚔️ For Nether explorers

If you explore fortresses, search bastions, or travel through the Nether, **stay alert**:

- **Dodge fireballs:** Ghasts reposition their aim more quickly, making open areas riskier.
- **Keep your shield ready:** carry it in your offhand. Fireball speed is unchanged, but attacks may feel more frequent.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="⚠️ Be careful in enclosed spaces">

A fast Ghast may appear and shoot before you can take cover when mining into a large cave. Build a safe nook as you explore.

</WikiCallout>

## 🔧 Compatibility

This datapack preserves vanilla mechanics. It does not change Ghast health, damage, or drops—only their movement and behavior. It works alongside other mob datapacks.
