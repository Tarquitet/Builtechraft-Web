## Launchers we do not recommend

<WikiCallout type="danger" title="TLauncher">

We do not recommend TLauncher:

- It is closed source and has a history of security controversies.
- It does not support Ely.by.
- It may cause conflicts with skins, accounts, and server settings.

If you use it, uninstall it and install Prism Launcher or ElyPrism from its official source.

</WikiCallout>

## Practices to avoid

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="Do not do this">

- Do not download launchers through short links, ads, videos, or unfamiliar repositories.
- Never share your Microsoft or Ely.by password; staff will never ask for it.
- Do not use modified installers or versions that promise advantages.

</WikiCallout>

## Ready to play!

Once the launcher is configured and your mods are installed, close the edit window and select **Launch**. The first start may take a little longer while files are configured. After that, Minecraft will be ready to play.
