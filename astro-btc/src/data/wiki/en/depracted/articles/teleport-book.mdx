Building a megaproject on a large map can be exhausting. Flying from your main base to a technical farm 20,000 blocks away can take more than ten minutes.

The **Teleport Book** datapack adds a special item to the Creative inventory. Save coordinates and travel between them instantly.

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="🚨 Critical rule: Creative mode only">

This item **does not exist** in Survival. Trying to obtain, duplicate, or use commands to get it in Survival is detected by Ledger and results in an **immediate sanction**. It is an exclusive benefit for builders and staff in Creative mode.

</WikiCallout>

## 🛠️ How to use it

The visual interface means you do not need to memorize commands.

### 1. Get the book

1. Open your inventory in **Creative mode**.
2. Search for `Teleport Book`.
3. Take it and hold it.

### 2. Save a point of interest

1. Fly to the location you want to save, such as a megaproject, distant farm, or spawn.
2. Right-click the air while holding the book.
3. In the interface, select **Save**.
4. Give the location a descriptive name, such as "Main Base" or "Slime Farm."

### 3. Teleport

1. Right-click anywhere while holding the book.
2. Choose a saved location from the list.
3. Select its name to teleport there instantly.

<WikiCallout type="info" title="💡 Builder tip">

For a project covering hundreds of blocks, save its four main corners. You can jump between them in seconds to inspect details.

</WikiCallout>

## 🎯 Recommended uses

- **Reviewing megaprojects:** builders and staff can check progress across the map without long trips.
- **Screenshots and cinematics:** move between the best camera angles instantly.
- **Moving resources in Creative:** transport blocks from a central storage area to a distant build site.
