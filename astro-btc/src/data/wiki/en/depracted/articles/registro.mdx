The server verifies your account when you connect. **You do not need to create or enter a password in-game.**

<WikiCallout type="info" title="📜 Historical note">

The video below shows our **old manual password system** through chat. It is a community classic, but **you no longer need to enter commands to join**. Keep reading to learn how the current system works.

</WikiCallout>

<WikiVideo youtube="V7660esF7Gw" caption="Video: Former login system (no longer used)" />

## 📖 The former system (historical)

In the server's early days, new players appeared frozen and had to enter `/register <password>` in chat. On later visits, they used `/login <password>`.

Although it prevented username theft, **it had several problems**:

- Players often forgot their passwords.
- Entering commands after every server restart was tedious.
- Typing passwords in Minecraft chat was never secure.

**That is why we removed in-game passwords completely.**

## 🚀 Current system: Alternative Auth

Builtechraft now uses an invisible verification system called **Alternative Auth**, available on Modrinth.

Instead of asking for a password after you enter the world, the server communicates **directly with your launcher** when you select Connect.

<WikiCallout type="ok" title="How it works now">

- There are no `/register` or `/login` commands.
- You will not be frozen while waiting to enter a password.
- The server uses **Ely.by** (Non-Premium) and **Mojang** (Premium) authentication to generate a secure token.
- If you are signed in to your launcher, you can join immediately.

</WikiCallout>

## ✅ Access requirements

<WikiAccordion title="1. Be on the whitelist (required)">

Before opening the game, **you must be approved for the whitelist**. Join our Discord and request access. If your username is not in the administrators' database, the server will reject your connection.

</WikiAccordion>

<WikiAccordion title="2. Configure your launcher correctly">

Once your whitelist is approved, your only technical responsibility is signing in to your launcher correctly (see <WikiLink to="launchers">Launchers and installation</WikiLink>):

- **Premium:** sign in with your Microsoft/Minecraft account.
- **Non-Premium:** create a free Ely.by account and sign in to it in your launcher (ElyPrism or XMCL).

The system checks your session, verifies your username against the whitelist, loads your skin, and grants access.

</WikiAccordion>

## 🆘 "Invalid session" error

The only error you may see is **"Invalid Session"** or **"Failed to verify username."**

<WikiCallout type="warn" title="This is not a ban">

It means your launcher's security token expired after being open for a long time. **The server is not at fault.**

</WikiCallout>

**Fix:**

1. Close the game completely.
2. Open your launcher's account settings.
3. Sign out and sign back in.
4. Start the game again. Your session will refresh, and you should be able to join.
