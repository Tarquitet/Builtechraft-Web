## 🌟 The spirit of Builtechraft

Builtechraft brings building, exploration, and engineering together. Our goal is to create a monumental world with room for creative freedom.

The rules protect each player's work and help keep the community calm, safe, and collaborative.

To support that goal, the server has two main worlds:

## 🏗️ Creative world

The first place you visit and a favorite among builders. This world is a huge workshop and blank canvas where you can fly, test block palettes, and design megabuilds without gathering resources.

<WikiAccordion title="Administrator-level tools">

- **WorldEdit (Java and Bedrock):** create spheres, copy structures, replace terrain, and complete hours of work in minutes.
- **Minimap support:** Xaero's Minimap can help you navigate a world full of large projects.
- **Waystone network:** place magical stones to create instant-travel points between your area and spawn.

</WikiAccordion>

## ⚔️ Survival world

If you enjoy earning your progress, this is your home. The server is **semi-technical**: traditional gathering matters, and engineering is welcome.

<WikiAccordion title="Featured mechanics">

- **Farm freedom:** iron, gold, and gunpowder farms are allowed. You can also play fully vanilla.
- **Immersion and roleplay:** sit on stairs and slabs for gatherings or a rest.
- **Synchronized skins:** Ely.by integration lets players see one another's skins.

</WikiAccordion>
